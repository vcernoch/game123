      How to play

      Click your blue unit, then a highlighted tile to move or an enemy with a yellow ring to attack. On a touch screen the first tap shows the path or a battle estimate and the second tap confirms it; the “Zoom map” button gives you bigger tiles. Enter a city to capture it. In your own city with no unit standing in it you can recruit, and you can build there at any time: walls (garrison +50 % defense), a market (+2 gold), barracks (cheaper recruiting), a watchtower (the city sees 2 tiles farther), an infirmary (+3 healing, stops plague), a temple (+1 gold, no revolts) or an academy (recruits start as Veterans). You only see the surroundings of your units and cities (horsemen and units on hills see farther); explored land stays on the map. An enemy unit that disappears into the fog leaves a faded, dashed marker with “?” where you last saw it (or the tile it walked off into); hover over it to see how long ago. The marker fades away after 3 turns or as soon as you see that tile again. On roads (brown paths between cities) units move twice as fast. Crossing a river (blue lines on tile edges) costs an extra move point – not over a bridge – and a unit attacked in melee across a river defends 25 % better. At the start of your turn a bar below the header sums up what the enemy did to you (units lost or wounded, cities lost) and the tiles of your losses are marked with a red ✕ until you end the turn. Sometimes a random event hits you at the start of your turn: a bountiful harvest brings gold, plague takes HP from units in and around a city, and a revolt costs a city without a garrison its taxes. A unit that steps next to an enemy it can see must stop there (zone of control, marked with a small red stop sign) – it can still attack, and moving away from an enemy is free. Attacking an enemy that other units of yours stand next to is a flanking attack: +10 % attack for each of them, up to +30 % (the counterattack is not affected). A unit on hills attacking a unit below it has the high ground: +20 % attack. A horseman that has already moved at least 2 move points this turn charges: +25 % attack – but not against a unit in forest. Spearmen are strong against horsemen; catapults smash city garrisons and ignore walls; a cheap scout (Sc) moves 3, sees 4 tiles far, but can't enter or capture foreign cities. Units gain experience in battle and are promoted to Veteran (★) and Elite (★★) – each rank adds 15 % attack and defense. A unit standing in your city that hasn't acted yet can be retrained in the side panel (warrior → spearman or horseman, archer → catapult, scout → horseman): it keeps its rank and share of HP, pays the price difference plus a small fee, and ends its turn. A unit you no longer need can be disbanded (✖ in the side panel or D, pressed twice): in your own city it returns 40 % of its price in gold, elsewhere nothing. You win when the AI loses all its cities. Every map has a number (shown in the side panel): type it into “Map number” in the new game dialog to play the same map again or share it, and after a game “↻ Same map again” restarts it with the same settings. Hover over a tile to see the movement path. Resources near cities count for the city whose territory they are in: a gold mine (gold nuggets) gives +2 gold per turn, horses (horseshoe) make horsemen recruited there 4 gold cheaper. Coloured borders mark city territory (up to 2 tiles around each city); a unit resting in your own territory heals +2 instead of +1. Click a known tile out of reach to give a move order: the unit marches there over several turns (dashed route with the number of turns) and stops when an enemy comes into sight. The ⚠ button (or T) shades the tiles that enemies you can see could attack next turn – darker red where several can reach. The “Army” panel lists all your units – ready ones first, then marching, fortified and done – with HP and where they stand; click a row to select that unit. Tab = next unit with moves left, Z = undo move (only for a move that revealed nothing new – no new tile or enemy), F = fortify (a unit that hasn't moved or attacked digs in: +25 % defense until it moves or attacks; Tab skips fortified units), Enter = end turn (if some units can still act, the button shows how many and asks you to press again; Shift+Enter ends right away), Esc = cancel selection or skip animation (a click on the map works too).
