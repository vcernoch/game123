    How to play

    Click your blue unit, then a highlighted tile to move or an enemy with a yellow ring to attack. On a touch screen the first tap shows the path or a battle estimate and the second tap confirms it; the “Zoom map” button gives you bigger tiles. Enter a city to capture it. In your own city with no unit standing in it you can recruit, and you can build walls, a market or barracks there at any time. You only see the surroundings of your units and cities (horsemen and units on hills see farther); explored land stays on the map. On roads (brown paths between cities) units move twice as fast. Crossing a river (blue lines on tile edges) costs an extra move point – not over a bridge – and a unit attacked in melee across a river defends 25 % better. Sometimes a random event hits you at the start of your turn: a bountiful harvest brings gold, plague takes HP from units in and around a city, and a revolt costs a city without a garrison its taxes. Spearmen are strong against horsemen; catapults smash city garrisons and ignore walls. Units gain experience in battle and are promoted to Veteran (★) and Elite (★★) – each rank adds 15 % attack and defense. You win when the AI loses all its cities. Hover over a tile to see the movement path. Tab = next unit with moves left, Z = undo move (only for a move that revealed nothing new – no new tile or enemy), F = fortify (a unit that hasn't moved or attacked digs in: +25 % defense until it moves or attacks; Tab skips fortified units), Enter = end turn (if some units can still act, the button shows how many and asks you to press again; Shift+Enter ends right away), Esc = cancel selection or skip animation (a click on the map works too).
